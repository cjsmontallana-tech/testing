Beyond Education: Discovering the GIST Experience.

My School, My Gateway: Life at Gateways Institute of Science and Technology (GIST) Antipolo

When people ask where we study, we proudly answer: Gateways Institute of Science and Technology, or GIST for short. Located in Cogeo, Antipolo City, GIST is more than just a private senior high school. Behind its name is a story of education, opportunity, and the determination to prepare students for a better future. To understand GIST, we must look beyond its classrooms and discover what makes it a gateway for thousands of aspiring individuals.

A School Built on Purpose and Opportunity

GIST has a history that goes back to October 19, 1994. It was originally established as EARN School Pasig, Inc. In 1999, the Board decided to change its name to Gateways Institute of Science and Technology. Since then, the institution has continued to pursue its purpose of providing accessible and practical education to Filipino students.

The name itself carries a meaningful purpose. A gateway represents an opportunity to move forward — whether toward employment, higher education, personal development, or a brighter future.

As a TESDA and DepEd-registered technical-vocational institution and senior high school, GIST focuses on preparing young Filipino men and women for lucrative employment in the shortest time possible and at the least cost. Through practical learning and skills development, students are given opportunities to build knowledge that can be applied beyond the classroom.

Discovering Life at the Antipolo Campus

Located at 2/F ARE-J Bldg., Brgy. Bagong Nayon, Cogeo Gate 2, Marcos Highway, Antipolo City, the Antipolo Campus serves as a learning environment for students with different goals and aspirations.

GIST may not be the largest or most extravagant educational institution, but its value goes beyond physical structures. It is a place where students attend classes, develop friendships, participate in activities, and work toward their individual goals.

The Antipolo Campus was established in 2002 and has continued to serve students in Rizal and nearby communities. GIST has also expanded its presence through other campuses, including Fairview, Mandaluyong, and Pampanga, extending its educational opportunities to more learners.

For the students of Antipolo, Cogeo is more than just a school location. It is a place where learning, experiences, and ambitions come together.

Different Strands, One Purpose

One of the things that makes GIST meaningful is the variety of academic pathways it offers. Through its Senior High School programs, students can pursue strands that align with their interests, abilities, and future careers.

ABM – Accountancy, Business and Management

ABM prepares students for opportunities in business, finance, accounting, and management. It develops their understanding of entrepreneurship, financial decision-making, and the responsibilities involved in managing an organization.

HE – Home Economics

HE focuses on practical skills related to cooking, baking, hospitality, and tourism. It provides students with opportunities to develop competencies that can be useful in restaurants, hotels, hospitality establishments, and other related industries.

ICT – Information and Communications Technology

ICT introduces students to the world of computers, programming, software, and technology. It allows learners to explore how digital systems work, develop technical skills, and understand how technology can solve real-world problems.

From writing code and developing websites to understanding computer systems, ICT challenges students to think critically and creatively. Although programming can sometimes be difficult, every successful output becomes an opportunity to learn, improve, and gain confidence.

Despite having different interests and career paths, these strands share one important goal: preparing students with the knowledge and skills needed for their future.

The Mission and Vision Behind GIST

GIST is guided by a mission that reflects its commitment to developing competent and competitive graduates through a holistic approach.

"GIST is a school with a heart developing competent and competitive graduates through holistic approach fulfilling the needs of society for a better quality of life."

Its vision further expresses its ambition:

"GIST is a prominent educational institution in the Regions for the development of globally-competent students equipped with positive values in a changing world."

These statements show that education at GIST goes beyond academic knowledge and technical abilities. The institution also recognizes the importance of positive values, discipline, responsibility, and personal development.

Being globally competent means having the knowledge, skills, and attitude to face opportunities and challenges beyond one's immediate community. It is about preparing students not only to succeed locally but also to contribute to a wider world.

Understanding the GIST Quality Policy

The institution also follows a Quality Policy represented by the letters G-I-S-T:

G – Global Standard of Education

I – In Demand System & Discipline

S – Satisfaction of Customers

T – Technical Advancement

Each element reflects an important aspect of the institution's commitment to education and development.

Global standards emphasize the importance of quality learning. An in-demand system and discipline highlight the value of structured education and responsible behavior. Customer satisfaction reflects the institution's concern for the people it serves, while technical advancement recognizes the importance of keeping up with developments in technology.

Through industry partnerships, additional training, and seminars, students are given opportunities to gain exposure to professional environments and understand the expectations of different industries.

This connection between classroom learning and practical experience helps prepare learners for the responsibilities they may encounter after graduation.

The Core Values That Shape the GIST Community

Beyond its mission and vision, GIST also promotes core values that guide the behavior and decisions of its students and members of the institution.

God-centeredness

Faith in God serves as a foundation for decisions and actions. It encourages individuals to seek guidance, practice faith, and approach different circumstances with a positive perspective.

Inclusion

GIST recognizes the importance of diversity, equal opportunities, and belongingness. Every individual deserves respect, and different ideas and opinions contribute to a more welcoming learning environment.

Social Responsibility

Education also comes with a responsibility to contribute to society. Caring for the community and environment, supporting accessible education, and promoting sustainability are important parts of creating a better future.

Teamwork

Success is not always achieved individually. Cooperation, accountability, communication, and shared responsibility help individuals work toward common goals while making use of one another's knowledge and abilities.

These values remind the GIST community that becoming successful is not only about what individuals achieve but also about how they treat others and contribute to society.

Beyond Classrooms: The Reality of Being a GIST Student

Student life at GIST is a combination of learning experiences, responsibilities, challenges, and achievements.

There are days filled with academic requirements, research activities, practical examinations, and deadlines. There are also moments when students encounter difficulties understanding lessons, completing projects, or balancing their responsibilities.

However, these experiences are part of the learning process.

Classmates become sources of support, teachers provide guidance, and shared achievements create memorable experiences. Whether it is completing a difficult activity, understanding a challenging lesson, or successfully presenting a project, every experience contributes to personal and academic growth.

For many students, choosing GIST is also a practical decision. Its accessibility, affordability, and focus on technical and vocational education make it an opportunity for learners who want to develop useful skills and prepare for employment or higher education.

GIST may not be perfect, but its purpose is reflected in the opportunities it provides and the experiences students gain along the way.

A Gateway to What Comes Next

Choosing a school is more than simply deciding where to spend the next few years. It is choosing an environment where knowledge, values, experiences, and opportunities can shape the future.

For those considering enrollment, GIST offers an educational environment that emphasizes practical skills, personal development, and preparation for real-world opportunities. It encourages students to work toward their goals while understanding the importance of discipline, responsibility, and continuous improvement.

As students of Gateways Institute of Science and Technology, we recognize that our journeys may be different, but we share a common opportunity: to learn, to grow, and to prepare for what lies ahead.

GIST is more than a place of education. It is a gateway to opportunities, a foundation for growth, and a starting point for a future built through knowledge, determination, and purpose.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers based on the official GIST website.

What programs does GIST Antipolo offer?

GIST offers Senior High School programs under two tracks: the Academic Track, with ABM (Accountancy, Business, and Management) and ASSH (Arts, Social Sciences, and Humanities); and the Technical-Vocational Track, with ICT (Information and Communications Technology) and HT (Hospitality and Tourism). GIST also offers Technical-Vocational courses and, for A.Y. 2026–2027, introduces four-year college degree programs in Bachelor of Science in Entrepreneurship, Computer Science, and Tourism Management.

Is the tuition free?

Yes! Students coming from public schools may qualify for free tuition, provided that they submit all the required documents and meet the necessary qualifications. Enroll at GIST and take the next step toward a brighter future!

Is the school uniform free?

Yes! School uniforms Type A & Type B are provided free of charge to enrolled students.

Is GIST a recognized school?

Yes. GIST is a TESDA and DepEd-registered technical-vocational institution and senior high school.

Where can I find the enrollment forms?

The official GIST website has enrollment forms for Senior High School and Tech-Voc.

What are the requirements and class schedule?

These can change each school year, so check the official admission page at gist.edu.ph/admission or ask the school directly.

Where are the Information Office and Registrar?

The Information Office is on the Ground Floor and the Registrar's Office is on the 2nd Floor of the Antipolo Campus.

Can I see the school before I enroll?

Yes. Browse the floor-by-floor rooms in We Offer and the photos in the Gallery, or visit the campus using the map on the Location page. You can also experience GIST firsthand through an on-site campus tour and explore the facilities in person.

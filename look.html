OUR STORY

ABOUT US

We are a team of aspiring developers building our skills, exploring technology, and working toward a shared vision. We proudly come from Gateways Institute of Science & Technology (GIST).

GATEWAYS INSTITUTE OF SCIENCE & TECHNOLOGY

GIST is a TESDA and DepEd registered technical-vocational institution and senior high school. Its goal is to prepare young Filipinos for lucrative employment in the shortest time possible and at the least cost.

A SCHOOL WITH A HEART

For 30 years, GIST has built a system on the belief that education should be accessible and affordable without sacrificing quality and integrity. From pioneering technical-vocational education to embracing Senior High School under K to 12, GIST keeps working to break down barriers to equal access to education.

In the next 2 to 3 years, GIST aims to become a college offering bachelor courses in tourism, information technology, and business administration.

HOW GIST IS RUN

  • A Quality Management System with regular monitoring and review of goals and objectives
  • Continuous development of education staff based on industry and community needs
  • A curriculum that is regularly reviewed to stay current, relevant, and responsive to industry

PRESIDENT'S MESSAGE

GIST seeks to produce graduates who are competent, moral, technically skilled, and purpose-driven. It believes that people want, above all, to improve their quality of life, and that education should be both attainable and achievable. Its legacy is competitiveness coupled with positive values.

JOSE O. MONTENEGROPRESIDENT AND SCHOOL DIRECTOR
